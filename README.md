# Silence Horseman

Two independent options for the Hallow's End Headless Horseman, as checkboxes in the addon options:

- **Mute his voice lines**: silences his voice files with `MuteSoundFile` (IDs from the community listfile).
- **Hide his chat text**: filters his yells, says and emotes out of every chat window.

`/shh` opens the options. `/shh audio on|off`, `/shh chat on|off`, `/shh status`, `/shh debug on|off` (prints each NPC yell with its speaker, whether it was hidden and the seconds since that speaker last spoke).

Written for the TBC Anniversary client (Interface 20506). The chat filter matches any speaker with "Horseman" in the name (the Headless Horseman and the Shade of the Horseman who yells in the zone), so it is English only.
