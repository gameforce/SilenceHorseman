# Changelog

## 0.1.0
- Mute the Headless Horseman's and the Shade of the Horseman's voice lines (50 voice files), as a checkbox.
- Hide their yells, says and emotes from chat, as a checkbox.
- `/shh` opens the options; `/shh audio on|off`, `/shh chat on|off`, `/shh status`.
- `/shh debug on|off` prints each NPC yell with its speaker, whether it was hidden and the gap since the last one.
